# TOEIC Listening Part 1 - Sample Script

## Picture A
*A: The man is sitting at a desk and typing on a laptop.*
B: The man is standing in front of a building.
C: The man is reading a newspaper.
D: The man is talking on the phone.

**Answer: A**

## Picture B
A: The woman is opening a window.
B: The woman is drinking coffee.
*C: The woman is writing in a notebook.*
D: The woman is making a phone call.

**Answer: C**

## Tips for Part 1
1. Look at the picture before listening
2. Listen for verbs (V-ing form)
3. Eliminate obviously wrong choices
4. Don't get stuck on one option
