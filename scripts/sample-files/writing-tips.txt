EMAIL WRITING - FORMAL VS INFORMAL
===================================

FORMAL EMAIL:
- Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name],
- I am writing to inquire about...
- I would appreciate your response.
- Yours sincerely,

INFORMAL EMAIL:
- Hi [First Name],
- Just wanted to ask...
- Let me know what you think.
- Cheers,

KEY DIFFERENCES:
1. Greeting: Dear vs Hi/Hey
2. Tone: polite + distant vs friendly + casual
3. Contractions: avoid (I am) vs OK (I'm)
4. Slang: never vs sometimes
5. Closing: Sincerely/Regards vs Cheers/Thanks
